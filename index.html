Welcome to Cashflow

Cashflow is your personal finance OS for freelancers. Track expenses, analyze spending patterns, set goals, and make smarter financial decisions with AI-powered insights.

📊 Dashboard

Your financial overview at a glance.

  • Stat Grid: Shows total income, expenses, net proceeds, and budget utilization
  • Budget Usage: Visual progress bar showing spending vs budget limit
  • By Category: Breakdown of expenses by spending category
  • Projection: AI-calculated estimate of your final balance this month
  • Recent: Latest transactions at a glance
📈 Charts

Visual breakdowns of your financial data.

  • Donut Chart: Category-wise spending distribution
  • Line Chart: 6-month income vs expenses trend
  • Heatmap: Daily spending intensity for the current month
⇌ Compare

Analyze month-over-month trends.

  • Month-over-Month Table: Compare spending across months and spot patterns
  • Savings Rate Trend: Track how your savings percentage changes over time
◎ Goals

Set and track financial goals.

  • Create savings goals (e.g., "Emergency Fund", "Laptop", "Vacation")
  • Set target amounts and deadline dates
  • Track progress with visual progress bars
  • Goals help keep you motivated and accountable
↺ Recurring

Manage recurring expenses.

  • Add recurring bills: Netflix, Rent, SaaS tools, Insurance, etc.
  • Set frequency: Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly
  • Auto-log: Recurring expenses are automatically logged on the specified day
  • Total recurring/month: See your total fixed costs at a glance
✦ AI Analysis

Let AI analyze your finances.

  • Analyze Now: Get a detailed breakdown of spending patterns and actionable recommendations
  • Weekly Check-In: Mid-week pulse on your spending
  • Category Deep Dive: In-depth analysis of specific spending categories
  • Anomaly Detection: AI flags unusual spending patterns automatically
＋ Add Expense

Manually log a new expense.

  • Amount: How much did you spend?
  • Category: What type of expense (Food, Transport, etc.)
  • Date: When did you spend it?
  • Note: Add context (e.g., "Lunch with client")
  • Recurring: Mark if this is a one-time, weekly, monthly, or yearly expense
⊕ Payouts

Track your income payouts.

  • Record monthly payout amounts
  • Add notes (e.g., "Client name", "Project")
  • View payout history over time
  • Helps with income trend analysis
≡ History

Complete transaction log.

  • View all expenses with dates, amounts, and categories
  • Delete transactions if needed
  • See duplicate expense warnings
  • Total spent displayed at the top
◫ Yearly Report

Comprehensive annual financial summary.

  • Year selector: Choose different years
  • Annual stats: Total income, expenses, net, savings rate
  • Highlights: Best/worst months, highest spending category
  • Monthly breakdown: Detailed table of all months
  • Download PDF: Generate printable annual report
⚡ Quick Add

Add expenses using natural language.

  • Type naturally: "spent $45 on lunch with client"
  • AI extracts amount, category, date, and note automatically
  • Review parsed data before saving
  • Press Q or click the ⚡ button anytime to quick add
🎨 Sidebar Features

Navigation and tools at a glance.

  • Hamburger menu: Click to expand/collapse sidebar
  • Light/Dark Mode: Toggle between themes
  • Export CSV: Download all data as spreadsheet
  • Backup JSON: Save complete backup of your data
  • Restore JSON: Upload a previously saved backup
  • Version: Current app version at the bottom
💡 Pro Tips
  • Use Quick Add for fast data entry on the go
  • Set recurring expenses so they auto-log each month
  • Run AI Analysis weekly for personalized insights
  • Check Charts to visualize spending trends
  • Use Goals to stay motivated about your savings targets
  • Export/Backup regularly to prevent data loss
  • The data is stored locally — your finances stay private
